## 1. COP31 collapses in Antalya — no deal on fossil fuels (LEAD)

The UN climate summit in Antalya, Turkey, collapsed on Sunday after
more than two weeks of talks — the longest COP in history and the
first major breakdown since COP15 in 2009. At the closing plenary,
COP31 president Murat Kurum said there was no consensus on the
"Antalya Pact": the draft made no mention of fossil fuels, so the EU,
Panama, the Marshall Islands, Norway and others rejected it. The
conference had been due to end on Friday 20 November.

## 3. Russian drone destroys apartment block near Kyiv; 3 killed

A Russian drone struck a five-storey residential building in Dymer,
Kyiv Oblast, overnight on 22 November, destroying a whole section
from the first to the fifth floor. Three people were confirmed dead
and about 14 were unaccounted for; nine people were evacuated and a
child was pulled alive from the rubble. A separate strike on a
grocery store in Vyshneve killed three more.

## 5. BepiColombo reaches Mercury after eight years

The European-Japanese spacecraft BepiColombo slipped into orbit
around Mercury on Saturday after an eight-year cruise; ESA
confirmed the arrival on Sunday, while reporting a glitch — the
thruster burn was shorter than planned after a software issue. The
two orbiters are due to separate on 9–10 December.
